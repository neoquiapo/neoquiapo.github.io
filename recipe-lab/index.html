Portra look (firmware)
The tone curve fitted to your Kodak Gold edit (lifted mid-tones, deeper shadows, whites a little lower). 100% = as fitted (the cards), 0% = no curve. Plot: grey diagonal = no change, thin = as fitted, accent = this setting. In the firmware build too.
B&W mix (firmware, Acros)
How much each colour adds to the grey value, like a filter on B&W film. More red darkens blue skies. The sum must stay between 0.8 and 1.2.
Film character (firmware)
Built into the film and added to the menu's Highlight and Shadow, in the same direction: plus brightens the highlights, minus deepens the shadows (both together = punchier). Grain scale multiplies the strengths below.
Tone (camera menu)
Highlight + brightens the highlights; Shadow − deepens the shadows.
Split toning (firmware, Portra)
Tints are added after the camera's white balance, so a warm tint survives Auto WB. Portra and B&W keep separate settings; Balance is shared.

Date imprint (firmware, DP2X)
Yellow dot-matrix ’26年11月18日 at the bottom right, as on DP2X Card 17: printed before the film look, so the film's toning, curve, halation and grain go over it (grey on Acros). On the camera it is the Date Imprint menu (was Auto Rotate; auto rotate is then always on). Preview date is taken from the photo (EXIF DateTimeOriginal, or the X3F's capture time), else today; a date typed by hand is kept until the next photo. The camera prints its own clock date; month and day under 10 get a blank instead of a 0. Soft glow: a halo in the same yellow (sizes in camera pixels; off = Card 17, 25 % / 5 px = Card 19; the preview uses the firmware's exact masks). Like film, the date belongs to the frame: on a vertical photo it runs up the side (the side follows the photo's orientation tag). On photos that are not 3:2 (crops) the date keeps the camera's size and distance from the corner, scaled by the photo's long side.
Grain (firmware, film modes)
Strength at each Grain/Sharp setting. 0 and above: no grain.
The slider still sets the camera's own sharpening too, which the preview does not show. Only 14 has been tested on the camera.
DP1 grain v1: the same strength in every shot, in Gold and B&W. The pattern repeats from shot to shot.

The preview models the camera from a Normal-mode photo: Sigma's 11 tone curves from the firmware, the stills colour matrix, B&W chroma removal, split toning and the exact grain algorithms (v2 on the DP2X, v1 on the DP1). The firmware builder gives the same bytes as the project's Python build scripts; with no changes it rebuilds DP2X Card 13 and DP1 Card 14 exactly. Tone and colour responses were calibrated against real DP1 shots (about 2–3 levels of error on a 0–255 scale), so treat the preview as close, not exact. The DP2X firmware uses the same tone curves and matrix, but the calibration has not been checked against DP2X shots yet. On the DP2X each film keeps its own settings; the build command lists only the films you changed from the defaults. Sharpness and noise reduction are not modelled. White balance: the camera's own preset gains and colour matrices (from its X3F files); Auto is exact only when you open an X3F.
